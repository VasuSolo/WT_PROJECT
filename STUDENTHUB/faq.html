Frequently Asked Questions

1. What is StudentHub?

StudentHub is an online portal where students can register, view events, manage their profiles, and receive college updates.

2. How can I register?

Open the Register page, fill in your details, and click the Register button.

3. How do I log in?

Go to the Login page and enter your registered email address and password.

4. Can I update my profile?

Yes. Visit the Profile page to view and update your personal information.

5. How do I register for college events?

Visit the Events page and click the Register button for the event you wish to join.

6. How can I contact the college?

Go to the Contact page and send your message using the contact form.

7. I forgot my password. What should I do?

Contact the college administrator to reset your account password.
